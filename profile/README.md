**Steady tools, built below the waterline.**

Keelapps builds administration and compliance apps for Atlassian Cloud, on
Atlassian Forge. The apps are closed-source; everything about them — products,
documentation, privacy policies — lives at **[keelapps.app](https://keelapps.app/)**.

## Support

Questions, bugs and feature requests go to the
[Keelapps support portal](https://keelapps.atlassian.net/helpcenter/support/),
or by email to [support@keelapps.atlassian.net](mailto:support@keelapps.atlassian.net).
Security vulnerabilities: [keelapps.app/security](https://keelapps.app/security/) —
never a public issue.
